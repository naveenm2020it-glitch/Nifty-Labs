Reference guide

Calculation rules

HLC ATM strike = strike with the minimum absolute |CE Close − PE Close| across all available paired CE/PE strikes for the selected date and expiry.
BEP (CE Close + PE Close) ÷ 2
S1 ATM Strike − HLC ATM PE Close
R1 ATM Strike + HLC ATM CE Close
S2 ATM Strike − (HLC ATM CE Close + HLC ATM PE Close)
R2 ATM Strike + (HLC ATM CE Close + HLC ATM PE Close)
Important: “HLC ATM” names the selected strike. These calculations use the CE and PE closing prices directly; High and Low are not averaged into support/resistance.
